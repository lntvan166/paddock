Put the herd
in your pocket.

One command on the machine the agents already run on. Nothing about your herdr setup changes, and paddock has no account of its own.

curl -fsSL https://trypaddock.vercel.app/install.sh | sh

  1. Keep it running

    paddock local

    The dashboard on 127.0.0.1, as a service that comes back after a reboot. paddock doctor says which herdr it reached.

  2. Reach it from the phone

    paddock start --for 2h

    A quick tunnel and a pairing code: needs cloudflared, no account. On your tailnet instead: paddock tailscale, with Tailscale signed in.

  3. Pair and pin it

    paddock pair

    Scan the QR, then Add to Home Screen and allow notifications: a push arrives when an agent needs you.
